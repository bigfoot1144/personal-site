Circuit Sunrise at the Makerspace

January 19, 2026

At 6:40 a.m., the workshop smelled like solder and coffee. We were building a sensor rig to track vibration in a tiny metal bridge model. The prototype looked chaotic, but it had that useful chaos where every wire tells a story.

We burned two boards, mislabeled one power rail, and accidentally graphed room noise as "bridge stress." Once we cleaned the grounding and moved averaging into firmware, the signal snapped into focus like a radio station suddenly tuned correctly.

By sunrise, we had a dependable stream feeding a small dashboard. Nothing fancy, just clear data and a stable baseline. Perfect first win for a project that started as a sketch on a napkin.
