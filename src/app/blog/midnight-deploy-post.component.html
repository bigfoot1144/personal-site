Midnight Deploy and the 3:17 a.m. Cookie

January 8, 2026

The release started quietly. CI passed, metrics were green, and every dashboard looked like a postcard from a perfect world. Then exactly twelve minutes later, users began logging out at random. The only clue was a tiny mismatch in cookie attributes between two environments that had looked identical all week.

We traced requests, diffed headers, and discovered one staging value had leaked into production during a hasty hotfix rehearsal. The bug was small enough to hide from tests but big enough to annoy every third session. It felt personal.

The patch was a one-line fix, but the lesson was bigger: "looks the same" is not "is the same." After that night we added a startup sanity check that validates auth cookie config before the app serves traffic.
