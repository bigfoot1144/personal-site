Weekend Side Quest: Tiny Robot Cartographer

February 1, 2026

This weekend project started with a toy rover, a cheap distance sensor, and too much confidence. The mission: map my apartment without crashing into table legs. It failed gloriously for the first three hours.

The robot kept drawing abstract art instead of maps because wheel slip was worse than expected. I switched to shorter movement bursts, added simple obstacle buffering, and fused each scan with the previous heading estimate. Suddenly the map looked like rooms again, not weather patterns.

Final result: a tiny bot that can build a rough floorplan and avoid most furniture with surprising politeness. Next version gets better localization and a dramatic startup sound for no practical reason.
